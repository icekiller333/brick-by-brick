3D v61.6

Nov 15, 2026 — round 18 feedback, actioned — warehouses, cards, hands

  • The Brick Sorter (separate warehouses) can't be bought until you've unlocked lead, silver, and gold bricks.
  • Brick-type cards now have an extra card: one clear "HOW THEY PAY" effects card, then the flavor — including the griffin's "I'm a griffin, not a bandit. I'd never take one while you were looking."
  • Clouds and tremors cards got the same split: a plain explanation card first, then the build-up and the joke at the end.
  • Hands keep at least one brick between them — a second hand won't grab the brick right next to another hand's.
  • Wall tremors are 10% stronger.
  • Two- and three-hand raids now get their own warning cards first, before any hand comes down — no more surprise triple hands.
  • Story cards and the victory screen now show a little graphic related to what's happening, not just text and a button.
  • Fewer clouds on screen (22 down to 19), and fewer background critters at once (5 max, 3 on low quality) — bats stop coming in twos and threes.
  • Silhouettes in the hole are black cut-outs of the main characters now: Edgar, the Elder, Bram, Nell, the Minotaur, Skarra, and Cinder.
  • In endless mode the "ENDLESS" label is gone, so it stops squishing the dollar amount.
  • The hands warning no longer shows a multiplier — it just says hands are incoming.
  • Still open: round 17's upgrade-cost multiplier is 5% per upgrade, not the 2% asked; round 16's drop bar sometimes missing on the first brick after you turn.
